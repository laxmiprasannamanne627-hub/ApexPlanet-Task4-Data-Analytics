# Sales Performance Analysis – ApexPlanet Task 4

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data, identifying business trends, and validating findings using statistical hypothesis testing.

Completed as part of the **ApexPlanet Data Analytics Internship – Task 4: Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation**.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories and cities.
- Compare average sales between male and female customers.
- Apply statistical hypothesis testing.
- Present actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- SciPy
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Dataset Overview
- Total records: 1,000
- Total columns: 12
- Period: January 2025 – January 2026
- Key fields: Order ID, Order Date, Customer, Gender, City, Product, Category, Quantity, Unit Price and Total Sales.

## Key Findings
- Total recorded sales: approximately ₹13.94 crore.
- Electronics was the highest-performing category.
- Laptop was the highest-selling product by total sales value.
- Patna recorded the highest city sales.

## Hypothesis Testing
**Business Question:** Is there a statistically significant difference in average sales between male and female customers?

- Null Hypothesis (H0): Average sales are equal for male and female customers.
- Alternative Hypothesis (H1): Average sales are different.
- Statistical Test: Welch's Independent Samples T-Test
- Significance Level: 0.05
- P-value: 0.4950

**Conclusion:** Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, we fail to reject the null hypothesis. The analysis did not find a statistically significant difference in average sales between male and female customers.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on maintaining strong Electronics category performance.
- Study successful products to identify opportunities for growth.
- Investigate city-level sales differences to improve regional strategies.
- Use customer and sales data to support better business decisions.
